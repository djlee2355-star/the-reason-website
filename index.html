Mission

Why The Reason (이유) exists

Rooted in Fairfax, our heart is to use music to encourage, serve, and support those in need—especially through local hospitals.

The Reason (이유) hosts benefit concerts and music events that direct financial support to local hospitals. We believe that guitar music and ensemble pieces can do more than entertain—they can bring comfort, hope, and a sense of unity to our community.

Through each concert, we invite listeners not only to enjoy live music but also to partner with us in generosity. Every note, every arrangement, and every event is designed to point back to caring for people who are walking through difficult seasons.

Our focus is Christ-centered, family-friendly, and community-oriented. We regularly serve at:

Community centers
Church & youth events
Nursing homes
Small gatherings

FAQ

Common questions

A quick overview for churches, schools, hospitals, and community groups who may want to partner with The Reason (이유).

Who is The Reason (이유)?
The Reason (이유) is a guitar-centered music group with additional strings, winds, and piano, based in Fairfax, Virginia and focused on benefit concerts for local hospitals.
What kind of events do you play for?
We serve at community centers, church and youth events, nursing homes, and small gatherings that align with our family-friendly, worship-based focus.
How far are you willing to travel?
We primarily serve Fairfax and nearby Northern Virginia communities. Travel outside this area can be discussed depending on timing and logistics.
Do you charge a fee?
Because our focus is on benefit concerts and serving, we usually receive donations or honorariums rather than a fixed fee. Details can be arranged for each event.
Is the music appropriate for families and churches?
Yes. Our selections are worship-based, Christ-honoring, and family-friendly. We do not play explicit or inappropriate content.
How can we book The Reason (이유)?
